Under the cards of [Server: Monitoring](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/server-monitoring), the **Over time** section shows charts of the <Term id="metric">metrics</Term>. Use them to find what happened when you did not look, for example a slow site at 3 a.m.

## Select a time range

Click **1 h**, **6 h** or **24 h**. Each point is the average of the readings of a step.

| Range | Step |
|---|---|
| **1 h** (default) | 10 seconds |
| **6 h** | 1 minute |
| **24 h** | 4 minutes |

## Read the charts

| Chart | Content |
|---|---|
| **CPU**, **Memory**, **Disk**, **Network** | The values of the cards. **Disk** adds the speeds of all the disks of the cards. |
| **Requests** | The requests of your services, their errors and their time to answer. See [Server: Requests](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/server-requests). |
| **Busiest services and datastores** | The CPU and the memory of the 5 containers with the highest CPU use in the range. The legend gives the color of each. |

Put the pointer on a chart to see the time of a point and its values.

A gap in a line is a time when Ferry did not run. When Ferry starts again, the readings of the last 24 hours stay.

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Collecting data">
    Ferry reads the server for the first time a few seconds after it starts. Until then, the section shows `Collecting data… first points in about 10 s`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not available">
    If ferryd could not read a value in the range, its chart shows **Not available**. On a Mac, this is the case for most values of the server.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The size of the history">
    The readings stay in the database of the <Term id="data-directory" />, `ferry.db`. With 20 containers, 24 hours of readings use about 15 MB. You cannot change the interval of 10 seconds or the time of 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When the charts change">
    The page asks for new readings every 10 seconds. If you go to a different tab, the page stops.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="API">
    The charts read `GET /api/v1/server/metrics/history?range=1h`. The range is `1h`, `6h` or `24h`. See [Server metrics](/docs/reference/api/server).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
